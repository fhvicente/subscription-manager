# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
People with a pile of recurring charges (streaming, cloud, gym, apps) who have lost track of what they pay for. They open SubTrack after seeing a bank statement that surprised them, usually on a laptop or phone at home. Job to be done: see every subscription in one place, get warned before renewals, cut what they don't use.

## Product Purpose
SubTrack tracks subscriptions, totals the monthly spend, emails reminders before renewals, and has a paid plan via Stripe. Success: a visitor understands "your money is leaking" in five seconds, signs up, and adds their first subscription without help.

## Brand Personality
Acid, blunt, funny. Three words: loud, honest, unbothered. The tone is a friend who points at your bank statement and laughs with you, then helps. Never scolding, never corporate.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS landing: centered hero, slate gradients, three icon cards, "Join thousands of users".
- Fintech navy-and-gold or neon-on-black crypto.
- Editorial serif + mono labels magazine lane.
- Neo-brutalist hard drop shadows.

## Design Principles
1. Make the cost visible: numbers are the hero, shown big and in real math.
2. One loud idea per screen; the app inside stays calm enough to work in.
3. Honest copy: no invented stats, no fake social proof.
4. Motion explains (counting, printing, striking through), never decorates.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA. All GSAP motion is disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`; content is fully visible without JS animation. Color is never the only signal (costs carry a sign/label, not just red).
